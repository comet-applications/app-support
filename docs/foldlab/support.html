Level & slope on iPhone and iPad

FoldLab opens directly to the free Level & slope screen on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later. Center the bubble to level one device plane, or align the long edge with a slope in portrait orientation to read pitch, grade and rise per 12 units of run. No purchase or account is needed. Wide windows show the instrument and supporting readouts side by side; narrow windows stack them. If readings do not arrive, FoldLab retries automatically and provides Retry motion.

Additional hinge tools

On iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later, FoldLab also offers live hinge angles, fold diagnostics and an outer-display companion. Move the hinge gently for the first reading. Devices without the required hinge hardware keep the complete free surface level and slope tools, without hinge-only tabs or a hardware requirement screen. Never force the hinge or use the device against sharp, hot, or powered equipment.

Save a reading

With Pro, tap Save beside Hold in Tools to keep the angle on screen, from any tool. While a reading is held, Save keeps the held value. Saved readings appear in Library with the tool that produced them and its result, such as a miter cut. Each input library keeps the latest 50.

Try the tools

On iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later, open Settings and enable Demo mode to use manual angle and tilt sliders. Demo records stay separate from live records. Advanced tools require Pro in both modes. The outer display companion appears only when supported and made available by the system.

Restore Pro

On iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later, open Settings → FoldLab Pro → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the price is unavailable, check your connection and choose Retry App Store connection. A pending purchase unlocks Pro only after Apple approves it. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. All supported iPhones and iPads can use the free level, pitch, grade and rise tools without buying or restoring Pro.

Photos and permissions

Reference photos are optional. If camera permission is denied, enable it for FoldLab in iOS Settings or use Choose photo. Deleting a saved reference photo does not delete the original in Photos.

Measurement limits

FoldLab readings can differ from the true angle by up to 5°. Readings are shown to 0.1° so small changes are visible, but displayed decimal places are not a guarantee of accuracy. Verify results with a dedicated instrument when precision matters. Joint / ROM readings are personal observations, not medical assessments. Hinge observations do not certify mechanical health. The bubble level measures one device plane.

Get help

Use the support link on FoldLab's App Store page. Include the app version, device model, iOS version, whether you used live or demo input, and the steps that led to the issue. Share reference photos only if you choose to.
