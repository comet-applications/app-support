Privacy Policy for Pigment Compass

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Pigment Compass helps you compare colors in reference photos with a miniature-paint catalog and organize your paints and projects. This policy explains what the app handles and the choices available to you.

No account or developer-operated service

Pigment Compass does not require an account or sign-in. The app has no developer-operated backend and does not upload your photos, inventory, wish list, projects, recipes, samples, or backups to the developer. Color matching runs on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information you create locally, including your paint inventory and wish list, custom paints, project names and recipe steps, sampled colors, and saved reference photos. This information remains in the app's storage unless you delete it, restore a backup, remove the app, or choose to export or share it.

Camera and photo library

Camera and photo-library access are optional. Pigment Compass requests access only when you choose to take or select a reference photo. Selected images are normalized for on-device use, and location and unrelated metadata are removed from the normalized copy used by the app. The camera does not run in the background.

Exports, backups, and sharing

When you choose to create a backup, import a CSV file, or export a PDF, the operation is initiated by you. If you then use the iOS share sheet, Files, AirDrop, email, cloud storage, or another destination, the selected file is transferred to that destination under its own privacy practices. A PDF can optionally include a reference photo only when you choose that option.

Device backups

Your operating system may include app files in device or cloud backups according to your device and Apple account settings. Pigment Compass does not control those system backup settings.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Pigment Compass does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove it. You can erase the app's user-created data from Settings using Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its local app container, subject to any device or cloud backups controlled by your operating system.

Children

Pigment Compass is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if Pigment Compass's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do. For privacy questions or support, use the Pigment Compass Support page. Please do not include personal photos, backup files, or other sensitive information in a public support request.
