Privacy Policy for Pigment Compass

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Pigment Compass helps you compare colors in reference photos with a miniature-paint catalog and organize your paints and projects. This policy explains what the app handles and the choices available to you.

No Pigment Compass account or developer-operated service

Pigment Compass does not require a Pigment Compass account or sign-in. The app has no developer-operated backend and does not upload your photos, inventory, wish list, projects, recipes, samples, or backups to the developer. Color matching and barcode lookup run on your device.

Information stored on your device and in iCloud

The app stores information you create locally, including your paint inventory and wish list, custom paints, barcode mappings, project names and recipe steps, sampled colors, and saved reference photos.

When iCloud sync is enabled, this information is also stored in your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit and synced to your other devices using the same Apple Account. Sync is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings > Backup and restore. An Apple Account and available iCloud storage are required. Pigment Compass does not operate a separate data server or send your collection to analytics or advertising services.

Camera and photo library

Camera and photo-library access are optional. Pigment Compass requests access only when you choose to take or select a reference photo or scan a paint barcode. Selected and captured images are normalized for app use, and location and unrelated source metadata are removed from the normalized copy saved by the app. The camera does not run in the background.

Exports, backups, and sharing

When you choose to create a backup, import a CSV file, or export a PDF, the operation is initiated by you. If you then use the iOS share sheet, Files, AirDrop, email, cloud storage, or another destination, the selected file is transferred to that destination under its own privacy practices. A PDF can optionally include a reference photo only when you choose that option.

Backups and sync

Your operating system may include local app files in device or cloud backups according to your device and Apple Account settings. Pigment Compass does not control those system backup settings. Independently exported .hhbackup files are sent only to the destination you select.

iCloud sync propagates edits and individual deletions and is not an archive of earlier versions. Turning sync off keeps existing local and iCloud copies. Restoring a manual backup replaces local user-created data and creates changes that may then sync to your other devices.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Pigment Compass does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove it. You can erase the app's user-created data from Settings using Delete All Data. This turns off sync and removes data from that device, but it does not delete existing iCloud data or previously exported files. Re-enabling sync for the same Apple Account can restore data from iCloud. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to any device or cloud backups controlled by your operating system. Use Apple's iCloud storage controls to manage data stored in iCloud.

Children

Pigment Compass is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if Pigment Compass's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do. For privacy questions or support, use the Pigment Compass Support page. Please do not include personal photos, backup files, or other sensitive information in a public support request.
