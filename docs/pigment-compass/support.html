Pigment Compass Support

Pigment Compass finds miniature-paint catalog colors similar to a color sampled from a reference photo and helps you organize your paints and project recipes.

Get help

Report a bug or ask a product question through the public support tracker. Include the Pigment Compass version, device model, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened.

Please do not post personal photos, backup files, contact information, or other sensitive data. Review and redact screenshots before posting.

Color matching

Results are comparisons against digital catalog references. They are a shortlist for experimentation, not a measurement of physical paint, identification of the paint originally used, or prediction of how a paint will dry. Lighting, camera processing, screen settings, surface finish, and paint application can change perceived color.

Photos and permissions

Camera and photo-library access are optional and requested only when you choose the related action. Matching and barcode lookup run on device. You can continue using the built-in demo image or type a paint name without granting photo access.

iCloud sync

Use the same Apple Account and allow iCloud access on each device. Open Pigment Compass on both devices; sync runs while the app is open and resumes when you reopen it. Settings > Backup and restore shows the latest completed sync, any problem, and a Sync now action.

You can keep working offline, and pending edits upload when iCloud is available again. If syncing does not finish, check your network connection and available iCloud storage. Sync propagates deletions and is not an archive of past versions, so keep dated .hhbackup files if you want independent recovery points.

If the Apple Account changes, sync pauses to prevent collections from different accounts being mixed. Export a manual backup before clearing local data and enabling sync for a different account.

Backup and restore

Use Settings and open Backup and restore to export an independent backup, restore a .hhbackup file, or import inventory from the supported CSV format. Restoring a backup replaces the current user-created data on that device and the replacement may sync to your other devices, so export a current backup first if you may need it. PDF recipe sheets cannot restore a collection.

Delete local data

In Pigment Compass, open Settings and choose Delete All Data. This turns off sync and removes your local inventory, wish list, custom paints, projects, recipes, samples, barcode mappings, and saved photos from that device. Existing iCloud data and previously exported files are not deleted; re-enabling sync for the same Apple Account can restore data from iCloud.
