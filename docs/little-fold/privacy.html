Privacy Policy for Little Fold

Effective October 25, 2026.

Little Fold is an offline dumpling-making game from Jared Kaczynski. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, require an account, display advertising, or include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information on your device

Your current round, chosen filling, scoop positions, folding progress, pleat accuracy, scores, recent dumplings, and sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. The steamer keeps up to 100 completed dumplings. We do not operate a gameplay server, account system, or cloud-sync service.

iPhone Duo and touch input

On supported iPhone Duo devices, the app reads hinge angle and open/closed display posture to fold the dough and move play between the inner and outer screens. Touch positions and hold durations determine which pleats you seal and their accuracy. These inputs are processed on the device. Resulting game progress and scores are saved locally; raw hinge or touch histories are not sent to us.

Permissions and Apple services

Little Fold does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier. Sound effects are generated on the device. Apple handles App Store distribution, TestFlight, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy practices.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. The app does not send support messages automatically. GitHub support issues are public; do not post personal information or account details there.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them makes a web request to GitHub, which may log visitor information such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress, scores, and settings remain until the app's local data is removed. Starting another dumpling resets the current round while keeping completed dumplings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. To request deletion of information you sent directly to support, email us. Public GitHub issues and shared files are also subject to the hosting service's and recipient's practices.

Children's privacy

Little Fold does not collect personal information from players of any age and has no chat, advertising, or social features.

Questions and changes

Contact razrsword@gmail.com with privacy questions. This policy will be updated if the app's practices change. See Little Fold Support for help.
