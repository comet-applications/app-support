Little Fold Support

Make dumplings, close the phone to fold them, and pinch the pleats shut. Little Fold is a free, offline iPhone game with no account, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Get help

Email razrsword@gmail.com or open a Little Fold support issue. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Accessibility feedback is welcome. The issue tracker is public; do not include personal information or Apple Account details.

Make your first dumpling

  1. Choose pork and chive, shrimp and ginger, or mushroom and cabbage.
  2. Tap the middle of the wrapper to add two centered scoops, then tap Ready to fold.
  3. On iPhone Duo, close the phone in one steady motion. The hinge folds the dough and the folded dumpling appears on the outside screen.
  4. Hold each of the eleven pleat dots until it fills. Pressing the center makes a tight green pleat. Off-center presses can make loose gold pleats and lower your seal score.
  5. When all eleven pleats are sealed, tap Steam it. Read your score and view completed dumplings in Steamer.

Regular iPhones

No folding hardware is required. On ordinary iPhones, use the fold slider or Fold it button. These controls are hidden on iPhone Duo, where the physical hinge folds the dumpling. Little Fold requires iOS 27 or later; Duo hinge controls require iOS 27.1 or later.

Opening during sealing

If you open the Duo while sealing, the inside screen asks you to close it. Close the phone again to continue pressing the pleats on the outer screen. Your completed pleats are kept.

Scoring and accessibility

Filling, folding, and sealing contribute 30%, 30%, and 40% of the total score. Two centered scoops, a steady fold, and tight pleats earn the highest scores. VoiceOver and Switch Control users have an additional Seal next pleat action. Settings includes sound and haptic controls; Reduce Motion follows your system preference.

Saved progress and privacy

Your current round, preferences, and up to 100 completed dumplings are saved on the device. The app does not sync them to a server. Starting a new dumpling keeps your steamer history. Read the Little Fold Privacy Policy for details.
