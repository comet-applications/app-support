Unfold: Dinosaurs

A little book of enormous discoveries: 15 dinosaur spreads and a clearly labeled Pteranodon bonus, with paper folds, approachable facts and playful imagined calls.

Open, fold and discover

Tap Open the book to begin. On a supported hinge device, opening the device raises the paper. On other supported devices, use the Fold slider to open and close the virtual book. Use the arrows or Inside the book to choose a creature. Come to life moves a paper detail, plays an imagined call when sound is enabled, and reveals an answer to the discovery prompt. Field notes contains the full facts and evidence links.

Sound, perspective and accessibility

Sound respects the silent switch. Your book settings control paper sounds, imagined calls, optional forest ambience, haptics and available camera tracking. Calls are creative inventions, not recordings of known prehistoric voices. Camera permission is optional; see the privacy policy. Reduce Motion disables extra perspective and flourish motion while preserving user-controlled folds, sounds and facts. Page buttons and native Field notes support VoiceOver and larger text.

Saved reading position

The book resumes your current creature. Turn the page each time I close the book is optional and off by default. All content is included and works without an account or internet connection; external evidence links need internet access.
