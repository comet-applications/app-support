Privacy Policy for Unfold: Dinosaurs

Effective October 25, 2026.

Unfold: Dinosaurs is an educational paper pop-up book from Jared Kaczynski. It does not require an account, collect personal information, show advertising, or include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The book and sounds are bundled with the app.

Reading information on your device

Your reading position and settings, including sound, haptics, reader preference and camera opt-in, are stored locally. We do not operate an account server or cloud-sync service. The app does not send reading activity to us.

Camera and perspective

Camera eye tracking is optional. When supported and enabled with your permission, camera images and face/eye position estimates are processed on your device to adjust perspective. Images are not recorded, saved to files, uploaded or used to identify you. Face and eye estimates are temporary and are not stored as a user profile. No biometric data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn camera tracking off in Your book, or revoke camera permission in iOS Settings. Camera capture stops when the book closes, becomes idle, backgrounds, or Reduce Motion is enabled.

Device motion and supported hinge readings also adjust the paper and perspective. These inputs are processed on the device; raw input histories are not sent to us. The book can be read without camera permission.

Other permissions and external links

The app does not request microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or advertising-identifier access. Educational source links, privacy and support links open external websites only when you choose them. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Apple manages App Store distribution, TestFlight, backups and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under your Apple settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. The app does not send support messages automatically. Do not send camera images or other sensitive information unless you intend to share them. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process request data such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no website analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Local reading preferences remain until you remove the app's local data; uninstalling removes that data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Temporary camera estimates are discarded as the tracking session changes or ends. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Children's privacy

The book collects no personal information from readers of any age and has no chat, social features, ads or account system. Camera tracking is optional and stays on device.

Contact and changes

Contact razrsword@gmail.com with privacy questions. We will update this policy if the app's practices change.
