Privacy Policy for Waffle Duo

Effective October 24, 2026.

Waffle Duo is an offline cooking game from Jared Kaczynski. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, require an account, display advertising, or include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information on your device

Your best order score and sound preference are stored locally on your device. The current cooking round is held in memory. Waffle Duo does not operate a server or offer an account or cloud-sync service.

Motion and iPhone Duo controls

The app uses on-device motion measurements to vary pouring and detect a quick turnover and return when flipping the waffle iron. On supported iPhone Duo devices, hinge and display information determines when the iron opens or closes and which screen shows the game. These measurements are used during play and are not saved as a motion history or sent to us. Touch controls provide an alternative on regular iPhones and iPads.

Permissions and Apple services

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier. Sound effects are generated on the device. Apple handles App Store distribution, TestFlight, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy practices.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. The app does not send support messages automatically. GitHub support issues are public; do not post personal information or account details there.

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them makes a web request to GitHub, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and the best score remain on the device until the app's local data is removed. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. To request deletion of information you sent directly to support, email us. Copies of public GitHub issues and any files you share are also subject to the hosting service's and recipient's practices.

Children's privacy

Waffle Duo does not collect personal information from players of any age and has no chat, advertising, or social features.

Questions and changes

Contact razrsword@gmail.com with privacy questions. This policy will be updated if the app's practices change. See Waffle Duo Support for help playing the game.
