Waffle Duo Support

Welcome to the Flip-O-Matic breakfast diner. Waffle Duo is a free, offline cooking game for iPhone and iPad, with fold-to-cook controls on iPhone Duo. It has no account, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Get help

Email razrsword@gmail.com or open a Waffle Duo support issue. Include the app version, device model, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened. Accessibility feedback is welcome.

The issue tracker is public. Do not include personal information, Apple Account details, or private files in a public issue.

Make your first waffle

  1. Read the guest's order. Pick a batter and mix-ins, then tap Fire up the griddle.
  2. Drag the pitcher over either plate and hold to pour. Drag bowls to sprinkle. Keep the entire batch on one plate and aim for the green zone around 72%.
  3. Close the iron. At the FLIP cue, quickly turn the phone over and back, returning to the starting position.
  4. Wait for the green READY lamp and the diner-bell ding, then open the iron to serve.
  5. Read your counter check, earn coffee mugs, and start the next order.

Regular iPhone and iPad controls

When no hinge is present, labeled Close iron, Flip iron, and Open iron buttons appear automatically. Tap the pitcher for a small pour and a bowl to sprinkle on the chosen plate. VoiceOver provides pouring and sprinkling actions for each plate. No Duo hardware is required.

iPhone Duo

Unfold to prepare your waffle on the inner screen. Fold shut to cook on the outer lid display. Flip the closed phone over and back at the cue, then unfold after the ready ding to serve. Duo features require iOS 27.1 or later; regular touch controls support iOS 26 or later.

Sound and cooking time

Sound follows the speaker toggle in the menu and the iPhone's Silent Mode. Check both and raise the device volume if the cooking sounds are quiet. Bubbling, sizzling, crackling, the ready ding, and different opening reactions follow the cooking state.

Cooking pauses while the app is inactive or Help is open. Keep the game in the foreground while cooking. The current round is not saved across an app restart; your best score and sound preference are saved locally.

Messes, doneness, and coffee mugs

Both plates accept ingredients, but using both in the same batch makes a mess when you close the iron and costs 500 points. A fresh order cleans the press. Opening too soon leaves an underdone waffle; waiting too long burns it. Recipe, pour, flip timing, and bake accuracy determine your score and coffee-mug rating.
