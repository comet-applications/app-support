Cordari Support

Cordari is your private concert passport for keeping upcoming plans, attended shows, artists, venues, setlists, photos, and memories together.

Get help

Report a bug or ask a product question through the public support tracker. Include the Cordari version, device model, operating-system version, and a description of what happened.

Please do not post passwords, sign-in codes, private concert notes, ticket barcodes, email contents, or personal photos and videos. Screenshots should be reviewed and redacted before posting.

Sign-in and backup

Cordari supports Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. Use the same provider account on each device to access the same Cordari account. Your original photos and videos are not included in Cordari account backups unless you explicitly choose an upload or sharing feature.

Media and ticket matching

Photo-library, camera, file, calendar, notification, and Apple Music access is optional. Cordari asks only when you open the related feature. Supported recognition runs on device when available, and manual show entry remains available when it is not.

Cordari Plus

Cordari Plus is the optional subscription tier for higher media-recovery limits, automation, advanced insights, premium notifications, and watermark-free sharing. Purchases and renewals are handled by the App Store or Google Play. To restore an existing entitlement, open the Cordari Plus screen and choose Restore purchases. To change or cancel an iOS subscription, use Apple's subscription-management screen.

Delete your account

In Cordari, open Settings, choose Account & safety, and select Permanently delete account. The screen offers an export-first option and requires you to type DELETE before the request is sent.

Privacy

Read the Cordari Privacy Policy for details about account data, concert records, optional media access, service providers, subscriptions, retention, and deletion.
