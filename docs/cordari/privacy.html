Privacy Policy for Cordari

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Cordari is a private concert passport for recording upcoming and attended shows. This policy explains what information Cordari handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Account information

Cordari uses Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. When you sign in, Cordari receives the account identifier and the profile information the provider makes available, such as your name or email address. Cordari uses this information to create and secure your account, restore your concert history, and keep your data available across your devices. Apple and Google process sign-in information under their own privacy policies.

Your concert archive

Information you add to Cordari may include upcoming plans, attended shows, artists, venues, dates, lineups, setlists, ratings, notes, usernames, friend connections, notification preferences, and import or matching results. Cordari stores a local copy on your device and may send account and archive data to Cordari's servers for backup, synchronization, social features, and the app functions you request.

New shows are private unless you choose another visibility. Cordari does not automatically publish your concert history. When you explicitly create a share card or shared link, the selected information is prepared for the destination you choose.

Photos, videos, tickets, and imported content

Access to your photo library, camera, files, calendar, and music library is optional and requested only when you open a related feature. Your original photos and videos remain on your device unless you explicitly choose an upload or sharing action. Supported photo, video, and ticket recognition is performed on device when available. Cordari may use embedded date and location metadata from media you select to improve matching; it does not request continuous or background location.

If you choose a server-assisted import, email-ticket feature, backup, or hosted sharing feature, the files, messages, or selected content needed for that request may be transmitted to Cordari's servers and service providers. Cordari uses that content to complete the requested import, backup, or share operation.

Maps, event information, and music services

Cordari uses Mapbox to display maps and may contact event, artwork, and music services when you search for or enrich a show. Those services may receive request information such as search terms, IP address, device or app information, and map interactions, and they process it under their own policies. Cordari does not use this information for cross-app advertising.

Notifications and device information

If you enable notifications, Cordari stores your notification preferences and a push-notification token so it can deliver reminders and account-related messages. The app and its service providers may process basic device, network, diagnostic, and performance information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

Subscriptions

Optional Cordari Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. The store handles your payment-card details. Cordari uses RevenueCat to obtain product, purchase, and entitlement information and associates the entitlement with your Cordari account so paid access can be restored across devices. Cordari does not receive your full payment-card number.

Advertising, tracking, and sale of information

Cordari does not show third-party advertising, sell personal information, or use your concert archive, photos, videos, or notes for cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on a device until it is removed through the app or the app is deleted. Account data is retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, protect the service, comply with law, and complete deletion or backup requests. To request account deletion, open Cordari's Settings, choose Account & safety, and use Permanently delete account. Cordari signs you out after the request and reports whether deletion is immediate or scheduled and whether a limited retention period applies.

Children

Cordari is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support so the request can be reviewed.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Cordari's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do. For privacy questions or requests, use the Cordari Support page. Please do not include passwords, sign-in codes, ticket barcodes, or private media in a public support request.
