Curio Courier Support

Curio Courier is a portrait packing puzzle from Comet Software Development. Pack unusual parcels, preview their interactions, and plan for the protection that leaves the van at each delivery.

Get help

Email razrsword@gmail.com or use the Curio Courier support tracker. Include your app version, iOS version, device model, and what happened. Accessibility feedback is welcome.

The issue tracker is public. Do not post receipts, Apple Account details, personal information, or unreviewed diagnostic files. The app never sends support messages automatically.

Free play and offline tools

Normal attempts are unlimited. Tutorials, campaign routes, generated routes, Daily Dispatch, local saves, and earned tools work offline after installation. Every offered route has a tool-free solution. Purchased Supply Credits require an available iCloud account and a connection when redeemed. Store availability depends on the build and Apple services.

Missing supplies or pending purchases

Open Store & Restore Purchases, retry pending purchases, and refresh the wallet. Do not buy again to repair a pending purchase. If a purchase awaits approval, wait for Apple's result. Keep the original iCloud account connected while a supply purchase is being fulfilled.

A pending purchased action can be retried using its existing recovery record. A recovered paid route may resume separately from your current ordinary progress. Contact support if a pending result does not resolve; include the visible error category, not a purchase receipt in the public tracker.

Restore and account changes

Restore Purchases refreshes permanent purchased looks using your App Store account. Supply balances and spending history use the private iCloud wallet; restoring cosmetics does not recreate spent consumables. Changing iCloud accounts suspends unresolved operations for the earlier account. Reconnect that account to finish recovery.

Ordinary progress is stored locally. Enable optional iCloud backups in Settings, or export a progress file before changing devices. Choose a backup explicitly when restoring. Two devices' coins and tools are never added together. Reinstalling without a backup can lose ordinary local progress.

Refunds

Request a refund through Apple's purchase support. Apple decides refund eligibility. Observed cosmetic revocations remove that look; refunded supplies may offset later grants. Free gameplay remains available.

Save recovery and data controls

If the app reports a damaged save, export the preserved data before choosing a new local game. Freeing device storage can resolve a save failure. Reset ordinary progress is separate from deleting iCloud app data.

Delete iCloud app data previews the private backup and wallet records that will be removed and requires a separate destructive confirmation. Outstanding purchases and purchased actions must be resolved first. Remaining credits and consumable history can become unrecoverable. Finished consumables are not replayed into a new wallet. Apple manages App Store purchase records and permanent ownership separately.

Accessibility, sound, and stories

Use labeled placement and rotation controls alongside dragging. Text follows iOS text settings; high contrast and reduce motion are available. Music, effects, and haptics have separate controls. Optional on-device story variations fall back to the complete authored text when unavailable and never determine puzzle outcomes.

Privacy

Read the Curio Courier Privacy Policy. Review a diagnostic export in Settings before choosing where to share it.
