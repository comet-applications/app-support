Privacy Policy for Curio Courier

Effective September 8, 2026.

Curio Courier is a puzzle game from Comet Software Development. This policy describes the app's local storage, optional Apple services, and support workflow. Some Apple features may be unavailable in development builds.

Information on your device

The app stores puzzle arrangements, route progress, earned items, preferences, and recovery checkpoints locally. If you enable local diagnostics, the app keeps a bounded journal of game and service outcomes for at most 30 days or 5,000 events. Diagnostics do not upload automatically. You can preview and delete them in Settings.

Apple purchases and private iCloud records

Apple processes App Store payments. The app receives verified purchase and entitlement information needed to deliver supplies and cosmetic looks. We do not receive payment-card information.

Purchased Supply Credits use your private CloudKit database for the balance, purchase grants, spending records, and purchased-action recovery. Optional progress backups also use your private CloudKit database, in a separate area. Apple handles the service and account information required for those requests. We do not operate an app server or a support console for individual private wallets.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to show occasional full-screen ads between completed routes and Google's User Messaging Platform to request and remember privacy choices where required. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not request access to the IDFA. Google's bundled privacy manifest nevertheless declares that another device identifier may be linked and used for tracking. Either permanent cosmetic collection removes ad breaks.

Google's advertising SDK may automatically process an IP address to estimate general location; crash and performance diagnostics; app- or developer-bounded device identifiers; ads viewed; and app or ad interactions. Depending on the data type, Google's bundled privacy manifests declare uses for third-party advertising, developer advertising, analytics, app functionality, and tracking. Processing is subject to the user's consent choices and applicable law. Curio Courier does not operate its own advertising or analytics server and does not receive a user-level advertising profile.

When Google requires a consent message, the app asks before requesting ads. Eligible users can reopen Google's privacy choices from Settings. Google's handling of advertising data is also described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional on-device stories

Where supported and enabled, Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework may vary fictional customer dialogue. Prompts use fictional game information. No remote AI fallback or transcript upload is used. Authored text remains available; AI never controls purchases, rewards, or puzzle outcomes.

Permissions and sharing

Notification permission is requested only when you enable a local reminder. Denial leaves reminders off. The game does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, Apple's advertising identifier, or photo-library read access. Google Mobile Ads may infer general location from the network address and use other app- or developer-bounded identifiers as described above. You choose save files through the system file picker and export files through the system share sheet.

When you choose to share a save, challenge, or diagnostic file, the recipient's privacy practices apply. Review files before sharing. Opening support or privacy links makes an ordinary web request to GitHub Pages, whose hosting practices apply.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. GitHub support issues are public. Do not include Apple Account details, receipts, or other personal information in a public issue. The app never sends email automatically.

Deletion and retention

Reset ordinary progress clears ordinary local gameplay while preserving purchased supplies, entitlements, and pending financial operations. Delete saved diagnostics removes the optional local journal. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them.

Delete iCloud app data separately previews and removes this app's private backups and wallet data after outstanding purchases are resolved and you explicitly confirm. Unspent credits and consumable history may become unrecoverable. Previously finished consumables are not recreated. This does not delete Apple's App Store records or permanent purchase ownership, which Apple manages separately. Financial idempotency records are retained locally to prevent accidental replay.

Optional progress backups retain the latest three per installation and twenty overall. Wallet financial records are retained while the wallet exists to support balance and recovery. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to Apple's device backup behavior.
